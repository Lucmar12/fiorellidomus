Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Domus Fiorelli does not use an automated booking engine on this website: every reservation is confirmed directly with the host via WhatsApp or email. As a result, cancellation terms — including any deposit, refund or rescheduling conditions — are agreed individually with the host at the time of booking confirmation and communicated in writing before payment.

If you have already booked and need to cancel or change your reservation, please contact Alessandro directly on WhatsApp at +39 349 351 1305, referencing your booking details.

If you booked through a third-party platform (such as Airbnb or Booking.com), that platform's own cancellation policy applies instead.
