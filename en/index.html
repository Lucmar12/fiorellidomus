Location

Appio Latino, the Real Rome

Live Rome by day. Return to the quiet of a real Roman neighbourhood by night.

Domus Fiorelli is located in Piazza di Villa Fiorelli, in the Appio Latino neighbourhood: a quiet, authentically Roman position between San Giovanni and Pigneto, with Rome easily within reach. Early-20th-century buildings, local markets, small shops, bars and neighbourhood restaurants reveal the real life of the area. From here, the historic centre and the main attractions are easy to reach.

  • Authentic residential neighbourhood, away from mass tourism
  • Elegant early-20th-century buildings
  • Local markets, bars and neighbourhood restaurants

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you want to know

How many guests can Domus Fiorelli accommodate?
Domus Fiorelli sleeps up to 4 guests, with 1 double bedroom and 1 full-size double sofa bed (21 cm mattress) in the living room.
What amenities are included in the apartment?
A fully equipped kitchen, washing machine, air conditioning, high-speed Wi-Fi and a Smart TV.
How far is Domus Fiorelli from the metro and Rome's centre?
Metro C (Lodi) is 600 metres away, Metro A (Re di Roma) is 750 metres away, and Roma Tuscolana station — with regional trains and a connection to Fiumicino Airport — is 550 metres away.
How does check-in work?
Alessandro welcomes you in person at Domus Fiorelli. Check-in is from 3:00 PM, check-out by 11:00 AM.
Is the website and guide available in multiple languages?
Yes: the website is available in Italian, English, French, German and Spanish, and a dedicated digital guide is also available during your stay.
